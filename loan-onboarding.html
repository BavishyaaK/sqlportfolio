Loan Onboarding Process Improvement

Digital Loan Origination | BFSI | Business Analyst

Reduced loan onboarding turnaround time from 7 days to under 8 hours by automating KYC verification, credit assessment, and approval workflows.

View Case Study Download BRD

Business Problem

A retail bank relied heavily on manual processes for loan onboarding. Applications were submitted through branch offices and routed via email to KYC teams, credit analysts, and approvers.

Challenges

  • Average turnaround time of 5-7 business days
  • Manual KYC verification caused delays
  • High volume of document rework
  • No application tracking for customers
  • Frequent data entry errors
  • Regulatory compliance risks

As-Is Process

The existing onboarding process required several manual handoffs and duplicate data entry across systems.

To-Be Process

The future-state process leverages automated KYC, credit bureau integration, risk scoring, and workflow routing for faster loan approvals.

User Stories

US-001

As a customer, I want to apply for a loan online so that I don't have to visit a branch.

  • Application accessible online
  • Mandatory validation enforced
  • Confirmation generated

US-002

As a customer, I want to upload documents electronically.

  • PDF/JPG supported
  • Max file size validation
  • Upload success notification

US-003

As a customer, I want real-time application tracking.

  • Latest status visible
  • Timeline displayed
  • Auto refresh available

US-004

As a KYC Officer, I want failed KYC cases routed for manual review.

  • Failed applications appear in queue
  • Reason for failure visible
  • Review status tracked

US-005

As a Credit Analyst, I want bureau scores retrieved automatically.

  • Credit score fetched automatically
  • Error handling available
  • Scores stored against application

US-006

As a KYC Officer, I want customer documents validated automatically so that I only handle exceptions.

  • Identity and address documents checked against the submitted application data
  • Passed cases move to credit assessment without manual action
  • Result and timestamp stored against the application

US-007

As a Credit Analyst, I want applications risk-scored and low-risk ones auto-approved within policy limits so that straightforward loans are decided without manual effort.

  • Risk score calculated from the retrieved bureau score and application data
  • Applications within the low-risk threshold are auto-approved
  • All other applications are routed to the Approver

US-008

As an Approver, I want high-risk applications routed to my queue with the KYC and credit summary so that I can approve or reject with full context.

  • Queue shows risk score, KYC result and credit score per application
  • Approve and Reject actions require a recorded reason
  • Customer status updates immediately after the decision

US-009

As a Compliance Officer, I want every decision and status change logged so that the process is audit-ready.

  • Each decision records user or system, timestamp and outcome
  • Log entries cannot be edited or deleted
  • Log can be filtered by application and date range
